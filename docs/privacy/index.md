# 案头 Workdesk 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 22 日

案头不收集任何个人数据。

- **你的数据存在哪里**：你记下的待办、分类与收藏只保存在你自己的设备上。如果你登录了 iCloud，它们还会通过 Apple 的 iCloud（CloudKit 私有数据库）在你自己的设备之间同步。这份数据属于你的 iCloud 账号，开发者看不到，也取不到。
- **不收集、不追踪**：案头没有账号系统，不含广告、统计或任何第三方 SDK，不追踪你在其他 app 或网站上的行为，也不会把任何数据发给开发者或第三方。
- **删除数据**：删除 app 即删除本机数据。iCloud 里的数据可以在「设置 → [你的名字] → iCloud → 管理储存空间」中删除。
- **儿童**：案头不面向儿童收集任何信息。
- **政策变更**：如有变更，会更新本页并修改上面的日期。

有问题可以在 [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Rainiehu/xiaoyu-workdesk/issues) 留言。

# Workdesk Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Workdesk does not collect any personal data.

- **Where your data lives**: Your todos, categories and favorites are stored only on your own devices. If you are signed in to iCloud, they also sync between your devices through Apple's iCloud (a CloudKit private database). That data belongs to your iCloud account; the developer cannot see or access it.
- **No collection, no tracking**: Workdesk has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. It does not track you across other apps or websites, and it sends no data to the developer or any third party.
- **Deleting your data**: Deleting the app deletes the data on that device. Data in iCloud can be removed in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.
- **Children**: Workdesk does not collect information from children.
- **Changes**: Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Questions: open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/Rainiehu/xiaoyu-workdesk/issues).
